The Collection & Recycling Lifecycle

Every electronic item is logged with an immutable audit trail from doorstep pickup to physical material extraction.

1

Register E-Waste

Submit item details, condition, and preferred pickup date. Instantly receive a unique RET-YYYY-XXXXXXXX tracking identifier.

2

Agent Collection

Authorized collection personnel inspect the item, verify initial weight, and securely transport it to the nearest consolidation depot.

3

Facility Intake

Certified recycling facility operators log receipt, perform non-destructive sorting, and categorize hazardous components.

4

Material Recovery

Safe extraction of copper, precious metals, and reusable plastics. Transparent evidence notes recorded with zero landfill contamination.

Accepted E-Waste Categories

We manage consumer and office electronics with specialized handling guidelines for each category.

💻 Laptops & Computers

Old desktops, notebooks, servers, and mainframes. Data sanitation and component harvesting are prioritized.

📱 Mobile Phones & Tablets

Smartphones, feature phones, and tablets. Safe battery removal prevents lithium runaway hazards.

⌨ Keyboards & Peripherals

Computer mice, keyboards, webcams, headsets, and gaming controllers for plastics granulation.

🔌 Cables, Chargers & Adapters

Copper wiring, power cords, and transformers stripped for high-purity electrical copper reclamation.

📺 Televisions & Displays

LCD, LED, and legacy CRT monitors. Specialized extraction of leaded glass and phosphors under sealed vacuum.

🔋 Batteries & Power Banks

Lithium-ion, LiFePO4, and NiMH cells. Critical minerals (cobalt, lithium, nickel) recovered safely.

Warning: Swollen or leaking batteries must be taped and packed in insulated containers before collection.
